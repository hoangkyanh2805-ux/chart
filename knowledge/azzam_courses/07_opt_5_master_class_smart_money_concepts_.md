#### Lesson 12.4: [Re-learn[ Total picture of Smart money concepts
In Forex, Cash exchanging need to do anytime at any price because:
Banks, funds always need to convert foreign currencies for profit purposes with deposits from people as an active source of income to pay interest on deposited money.
Countries or global companies always need foreign currencies to import products or use them for multinational operations.

In Crypto or Stock market, big funds always want to manipulate market to create phases to accumulate or distribute their assets to optimize their gains.
Totally, that is Smart Money – Huge money. Anytime they join in the market always create the “Footprint” to follow.

Firstly, we need to understand some phase they will do in the market.
Phase 0. They normally join to catch the bottom or Top when momentum of market already weak. At this phase we cannot confirm 100% and just wait normally because Smart money will buy a little to test whether Momentum is really weak to BUY. (I am giving an example in case of down trend market).
So, we normally skip this phase and use next phase as confirmation signals.

Phase 1. Smart money jump into market strongly after testing successfully. IMBALANCE will be created (as you see on the picture below). That is first confirmation.

Phase 2. Increasing too fast will cause “Lack of liquidity” – Smart money will push market go down with signals that traders call “Over bought”. They manipulate market and take liquidity as I shared on last post.

Phase 3. They (Big friends) almost have enough liquidity from sellers to fill their “empty gourd”. And, Price also came into DISCOUNT ZONE – Strongest BUYING zone.

Phase 4. Both Smart money and Retail traders will come and push market go UP strongly than the first time after all the signals defined obviously.

That is the time we will wait to entry follow Smart money. That also the first waves as Elliot waves.

What will we do after that phase?
Normally, After First waves, price will not come back to discount zone because missed chances people also join to catch the pullback. At this time, we select to entry with Key level will be more optimize than SMC strategy even we have IMBALANCE zone.
